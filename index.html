Official 201 Pool Rummy Rules

1. Objective: Arrange 13 cards into valid sequences and sets, keeping unmelded deadwood penalty low.

2. First Life (Pure Sequence): At least one sequence of 3 or more consecutive cards of the exact same suit with NO Jokers. This is mandatory!

3. Closed Wild Joker Vault: The cut Wild Joker remains locked inside the Vault until you form your Pure Life (First Life). Once unlocked, you can see the card and all identical ranks act as Jokers.

4. Second Sequence: A second sequence of 3 or more cards, pure or impure (using Jokers).

5. Drop Points: First Drop = 20 points (before drawing on turn 1). Middle Drop = 40 points (subsequent turns). Wrong Declaration = 80 points.

6. Elimination: Any player reaching or exceeding 201 points is eliminated from the tournament.
