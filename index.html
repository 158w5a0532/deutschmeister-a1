201 Pool Rummy Guide

Pure Life (1st Sequence): At least one sequence of 3+ consecutive cards of the exact same suit with no jokers.

Closed Wild Joker Vault: The cut Joker is locked in the Vault. It reveals only when your hand forms a Pure Life!

Natural Physical Controls: Drag any card anywhere in your hand to reorder. Drag a card directly to the Discard Pile to discard, or tap a card to see its instant discard chip.

First Drop (20 pts): Drop before drawing on turn 1.

Middle Drop (40 pts): Drop before drawing on subsequent turns.

Elimination (201 pts): Any player reaching or exceeding 201 points is eliminated.
